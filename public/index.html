Mystery Shopper

Your travel app's AI concierge recommends the same famous places to everyone, and sometimes places that don't exist. Mystery Shopper sends it test shoppers built from Qloo audience segments, checks every recommendation against Qloo's taste graph, digs into the worst segment, then repairs the assistant and re-tests it.

What this does and doesn't establish. Segments are aggregate audiences (a city, an age band, two taste seeds), not people. Qloo results describe what groups tend to like, not what any one traveller will do. PHANTOM means Qloo's catalogue has no match, which is strong evidence but not proof that a place doesn't exist. The headline checks (phantom, elsewhere, mainstream) are factual lookups. Qloo affinity is shown separately and labelled, because the stage-3 fix itself uses Qloo. The stage-2 fix uses no Qloo at all, so its lift can't come from grading with the fix.
